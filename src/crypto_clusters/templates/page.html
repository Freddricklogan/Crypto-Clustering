1. Choosing k, by two stated rules

K-means was run for k = 1 to 11 with ten initialisations each. The knee of the inertia curve (largest distance below the chord) picks k = $elbow; the highest silhouette picks k = $best (silhouette $sil_best against $sil_elbow at the elbow). At k = $elbow, $singletons cluster(s) contain a single coin — outliers the method isolates rather than groups.

3. HDBSCAN: a method allowed to say "noise"

With a minimum cluster size of $hdb_min, HDBSCAN finds $hdb_clusters dense clusters and leaves $hdb_noise coins unassigned (silhouette over assigned points: $hdb_sil). K-means must place every coin; a density method need not, which suits a small, heavy-tailed table.
